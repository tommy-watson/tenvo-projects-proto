The rules of the estate

What a covenant is,
and what applies to your stage

Different stages at Wharparilla carry different covenants. Pick which set applies to the block you're considering.

In plain English

A covenant is a set of promises attached to the land

When you buy a lot at Wharparilla, the title carries a restrictive covenant registered with the plan of subdivision. It's a list of things every owner agrees not to do with their block: what can be built, what it's made of, what can be kept on it. It exists to protect the look and value of the estate, so the home next door can't be a tin shed on a bare block.

It runs with the land, so it binds you and whoever buys from you later. The developer, Wharparilla West Pty Ltd, keeps the right to vary it for other lots in the plan. Your conveyancer walks you through it; the full memorandum is in the Section 32.

Applies to Stages 7A – 28 · summary of the registered covenant

What it means for your block

  • One home, 200m² minimum. A single private residence with at least 200m² inside the outer walls (garage, eaves, verandahs and alfresco don't count). Garage or carport built at the same time as the house.
  • Materials. No unfinished fibro or unpainted weatherboard, no reflective cladding. Roofs in non-reflective Colorbond, concrete, slate or terracotta tiles.
  • Keep the street clean. Air conditioners, tanks, hot water units, clotheslines, pool equipment, aerials and bins not visible from the front boundary.
  • Sheds after the house, not before. Outbuildings up to 180m² floor area, 3.9m walls and 5m overall, fully enclosed, in brick, stone or Colorbond (Woodland Grey or Monument, one colour), roof pitch 10–16°, inside the marked outbuilding envelope.
  • Garage and carport match the house materials and roof pitch; no carport forward of the front building line.
  • Fencing. No fence on the road frontage or forward of the house. Internal fences up to 1.8m in Woodland Grey Colorbond. Boundary fences post-and-wire to 1.2m, plus standard pool and tennis-court fencing.
  • Not allowed. Living in a caravan, shed or mobile home on the block; shipping containers; above-ground pools; second-hand or relocated buildings; subdividing the lot.
  • Boats, caravans and trailers stored in the garage or shed, not on the drive, street or nature strip.
  • Animals. Up to two dogs, two cats and six chickens or birds. No goats, pigs, horses, cattle, sheep, roosters or pigeons.
  • Use. Private residence only: no Airbnb, B&B or holiday letting, no business or commercial activity, no heavy or articulated vehicles.
  • Trees. Developer plantings stay; native vegetation needs council approval to remove.
  • No deadline to build is set in this covenant.

Plain-English summary of the Memorandum of Common Provisions for Stages 7A – 28, lodged by Melville & Hicks Lawyers. The registered document prevails.

Part seven · Questions we get asked

Straight answers

Can I put a shed up before I build the house?

In Stages 8 and 9, no. The covenant doesn't allow an outbuilding to go up before the home. Once the house is built, a shed up to 180m² is fine within the outbuilding envelope. Stages 1 and 2 to be confirmed.

Is there a time limit to start building?

The Stage 8 and 9 covenant doesn't set one. Check with us on anything in the contract of sale, and confirm for Stages 1 and 2. Confirm

Can I fence the block straight after settlement?

Boundary fences can go in as post-and-wire up to 1.2m. Internal fences (up to 1.8m Woodland Grey Colorbond) can't be on the road frontage or forward of where the house will sit, so most people fence the rear and sides first.

Can I keep horses or other animals?

Not horses, cattle, sheep, goats or pigs. Up to two dogs, two cats and six chickens or birds are fine (no roosters or pigeons).

Are there body corporate or owners corporation fees?

No. Lots are freehold with a covenant, not an owners corporation. Council rates apply as normal. Confirm

Is the estate in a flood zone?

[Confirm overlay status per lot with Campaspe Shire; refer to Section 32] Confirm

When will Stage 8 titles be issued?

Construction is nearly complete. Once finished it is lodged with council for compliance, then titles register. We'll give you the latest timing when you enquire.

Can I buy now and build in a few years?

Yes. There's no build deadline in the Stage 8 and 9 covenant. You can't live on the block in a caravan or shed while you wait, though.

Who are the developers?

Wharparilla West Pty Ltd, with Chris Smith & Associates (Shepparton) as surveyors and engineers and Melville & Hicks Lawyers (Echuca) handling the plan and covenants. [Developer background to add] Confirm

Can I walk the block before I decide?

Yes. Tom or Troy will meet you on site, walk the lot boundaries with you and talk through orientation, the reserve and the neighbouring stages.
